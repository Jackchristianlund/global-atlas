# Permission request — ISW / CTP front line assessments

Send to the Institute for the Study of War. Their site lists contact details;
the Critical Threats Project at AEI is a co-author of this dataset and may need
to be copied. Keep the reply: it is the document that unblocks a paid tier.

**Subject:** Permission to display ISW/CTP front line assessments in a paid product

---

Dear Institute for the Study of War,

I am building Global Atlas, an interactive map of current armed conflicts. It
shows assessed territorial control and front line geometry alongside documented
incidents, with every assessment carrying its source and its date on screen.

I would like your written position on one specific use.

**What I use.** The front line polylines published by UN OCHA at
`gis.unocha.org/server/rest/services/Hosted/UKR_Front_Line/FeatureServer/0`,
and the dated monthly series in the companion `UKR_Front_Line_FLC` service.
Both name ISW and AEI's Critical Threats Project as the assessing authors.

**How I use it.** The geometry is drawn as a map layer, unaltered except for
the generalization the service itself performs when asked. Beside it the map
states, permanently and not in a footnote:

- that the assessment is yours, naming ISW, CTP and OCHA;
- the date of the assessment and how many days old it is;
- that it is an assessed line of contact and not a real-time military position;
- that your data does not label which side of the line holds which ground, so
  the map does not either.

I do not redraw your line, infer positions between your assessments, or present
any part of it as my own work.

**What I am asking.** Global Atlas has a free tier and I intend to add a paid
one at USD 3.99 per month. The paid tier would not gate your data behind
payment; the question is whether a product that charges for anything may
include your assessments at all.

Specifically:

1. May this geometry be displayed in a product with a paid tier, and on what
   terms?
2. Is the attribution above sufficient, or do you require particular wording?
3. Are there limits I should observe — on how far back the archive may go, on
   derived figures such as percentage of territory held, or on bundling a
   stored copy for offline use rather than querying your publisher live?

If the answer is that commercial use is not permitted, I would rather know now
and remove the layer than find out later. If there is a licence to sign or a
fee, I would like to understand it.

I am happy to send a link to the running map so you can see exactly how your
work is presented before you decide.

Thank you for making this work public.

Yours sincerely,

Jack Christian Lund
